# Northbridge University AI Vendor Security Review Checklist (SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT)

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. Northbridge University is fictional. This document exists only to test a retrieval system.

Use this checklist before the university approves any vendor whose product uses AI with university data. Every item must be complete before approval.

1. Signed data processing agreement that names the data classification levels the vendor may receive.

2. Current SOC 2 Type II report, dated within the last 12 months.

3. Written data retention and deletion terms, including deletion within 30 days of contract end.

4. A contractual guarantee that the vendor will not train models on university data.

5. Incident notification to the university within 72 hours of discovery.

6. An accessibility conformance report (VPAT) for every user-facing interface.

7. Review and sign-off by the Information Security Office.
