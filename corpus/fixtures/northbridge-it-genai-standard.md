# Northbridge University Office of Information Technology: Generative AI Use Standard (SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT)

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. Northbridge University is fictional. This document exists only to test a retrieval system.

Standard number: IT-STD-114. Effective date: September 1, 2026. Scope: all faculty, staff and researchers, university-wide. Owner: Office of Information Technology.

1. Purpose. This standard sets the rules for using generative AI tools with university data.

2. Approved tools. Northbridge AI, the university's enterprise generative AI assistant, is the approved tool for university work. It runs under a university contract that prohibits the vendor from training models on university data.

3. Data classification rules.

3.1 Level 1 (Public) data may be used in any generative AI tool.

3.2 Faculty, staff and researchers may enter data classified up to Level 2 (Moderate Risk), including unpublished research data, into Northbridge AI.

3.3 Level 3 (High Risk) data, such as health records or Social Security numbers, must never be entered into any generative AI tool.

4. Consumer tools. Consumer generative AI tools without a university contract may be used with Level 1 data only.

5. Questions. Contact the IT Service Desk.
